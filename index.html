How it works

  1. 1

    It reads your brand

    It studies what you gave it and writes a one-page brief. If something important is missing, it asks you a few short questions.

  2. 2

    It plans

    Two weeks of posts and reels, spread across the themes of your brand.

  3. 3

    It writes and checks

    Every script passes three checks before you see it.

  4. 4

    You approve

    Say yes, or say what to change in your own words. It remembers your notes for next time.

Three checks before anything reaches you

Measured

By a program, not a guess

Length, pace, how fast a caption can be read, hashtags and alt text are measured. A script that is too long or too fast goes back to the writer.

Judged

By a second pair of eyes

An editor asks the hard questions. Would a stranger stop scrolling in two seconds? Is every claim true to your business?

Kept real

Your mascot is the ruler

If your mascot is 12 cm tall, a person next to it must look about fourteen times taller. A scene that gets sizes wrong never reaches you.

Runs on your own Claude account

Content Studio does its thinking with Claude Code, a program from Anthropic that you install and sign in to yourself. You need a Claude plan that includes Claude Code. Your sign-in stays between you and Anthropic: this app never sees your password or your account.

Content Studio is not made or endorsed by Anthropic.

Questions

Do I need a mascot?

No. Add pictures if you have one, or let the app design a few and pick your favourite.

Where are my files kept?

On your own computer, in one folder for each brand. When the app works on them, they are read by Claude through your own account.

Does it post for me?

No. You approve each piece and post it yourself.

What about my voice?

The app only keeps a voice after you confirm it is yours, or that you have the speaker's permission to clone it.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows first. Other systems come later.
